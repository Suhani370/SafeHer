Core Features

🚨 Emergency SOS

Quick emergency activation with a deliberate hold action.

🛡️ Guardian Dashboard

A centralized safety dashboard for important protection tools.

👥 Guardian Circle

Manage trusted emergency contacts for safety situations.

🧭 Smart Journey

Monitor a journey and confirm safe arrival.

⏱️ Safety Timer

Set a safety countdown with emergency escalation when required.

📍 Nearby Emergency Help

Find nearby emergency resources with calling and directions.

🔒 Discreet Mode

A discreet interface designed for situations where privacy matters.

🎙️ Audio Evidence

Safety-oriented audio evidence functionality.
